A parallel kitchen layout features two long counter runs running parallel to each other with a central walkway. Renowned by professional chefs as the most ergonomically efficient kitchen layout, it provides maximum counter space, abundant storage, and a natural separation between wet preparation and dry cooking zones.

Why Choose a Parallel Modular Kitchen for Your Bangalore Home?

When planning a kitchen for high-rise apartments or rectangular floor spaces across Bangalore, a parallel / galley layout offers unmatched practical advantages:

  • Superior Workspace Ergonomics: Creates a compact, highly efficient work triangle between the sink on one counter and the hob/refrigerator on the opposite counter, minimizing walking steps during cooking.
  • Maximum Countertop Area: Dual running counters provide ample space for small kitchen appliances (mixers, wet grinders, coffee makers, air fryers) without crowding your prep zone.
  • Clear Wet & Dry Segregation: Easily separates wet zones (washing sink, dish drying, water purifier) on one side from dry zones (cooking hob, spice racks, prep area) on the other side.
  • Abundant Storage Capacity: Offers two full walls of overhead wall cabinets, base tandem drawers, and option for a full-length tall pantry unit.
  • Ideal for Heavy Daily Cooking: Perfect for Indian homes where multiple dishes are cooked simultaneously and heavy cookware requires organized storage.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the minimum walkway width required for a parallel modular kitchen?

A parallel kitchen requires a minimum central walkway width of 3 to 4 feet (36 to 48 inches) between the two facing counters to allow comfortable movement and opening of opposite cabinet doors simultaneously.

Why is a parallel kitchen layout considered the best for cooking efficiency?

It offers the shortest distance between the sink, cooking hob, and refrigerator. With two facing counter runs, you can turn around from prep to cooking in a single step without walking across a large room.

Which core material is best for parallel kitchens in Indian homes?

IS-710 Grade Boiling Water Proof (BWP) Marine Plywood is the best material. It offers 100% resistance to water near sink zones, handles steam from heavy cooking, and prevents termite infestation.

Can a parallel kitchen fit into a small 2 BHK apartment in Bangalore?

Yes. Parallel kitchens are ideal for narrow, rectangular room layouts commonly found in 2 BHK and 3 BHK apartment floor plans across Bangalore.

How long does it take to manufacture and install a parallel modular kitchen?

Factory manufacturing takes 25 to 30 days post 3D design approval. On-site installation, countertop fitting, and hardware calibration typically take 3 to 5 business days.

What warranty do you provide on parallel modular kitchens?

Sunrise Interiors provides a 5-year structural warranty on all BWP Plywood cabinetry, along with manufacturer warranties up to 10 years on soft-close European hardware fittings from Hettich, Blum, and Hafele.

Can built-in appliances be integrated into a parallel kitchen design?

Yes. Our layouts accommodate built-in hobs, high-suction chimneys (1200–1500 m³/hr), built-in microwave/OTG tall units, and under-counter dishwashers.

How do factory-made parallel kitchens compare to carpenter-built kitchens?

Factory-made modular units use automated CNC cutting and zero-gap edge-banding. This ensures exact fitting, clean seals against moisture penetration, and fast installation without dust or mess at your home.

Can one side of a parallel kitchen be made into an open breakfast counter?

Yes. In open-plan apartments, the outer counter run can be designed as a half-height partition or island counter that functions as a breakfast bar facing the living/dining area.
