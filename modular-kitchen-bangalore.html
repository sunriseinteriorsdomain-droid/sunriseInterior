Popular Modular Kitchen Layouts Designed for Bangalore Homes

Our kitchen interior decorators in Bangalore curate layout configurations around the ergonomic Sink-Hob-Refrigerator work triangle:

1. L-Shaped Modular Kitchen

The most popular layout for apartments across Whitefield, HSR Layout, and Electronic City. It utilizes corner space efficiently while leaving ample floor area for movement.

2. Parallel / Galley Modular Kitchen

Featuring two facing counters, this layout separates wet prep (sink/washing) from dry zones (cooking/hob). It is the preferred layout for home chefs needing maximum counter space.

3. U-Shaped Modular Kitchen

Offers three full sides of cabinetry and storage, making it perfect for independent homes in Rajajinagar, Malleshwaram, and Jayanagar that require deep storage for cookware and provisions.

4. Straight & Island Kitchens

Designed for open-plan living rooms or studio apartments. Island counter extensions double as a breakfast bar, serving space, or casual dining zone.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Which material is best for a modular kitchen in Bangalore?

Boiling Water Proof (BWP) Marine Plywood with IS-710 certification is the ideal material for Bangalore homes. It offers superior resistance to moisture, humidity near sink areas, and termite infestation compared to standard MDF or particle board.

How long does it take to manufacture and install a modular kitchen?

Once the 3D design and material selections are finalized, automated factory manufacturing takes approximately 25 to 30 days. Final on-site assembly and fitting by our technical team typically take 3 to 5 business days.

What modular kitchen layouts are suitable for small Bangalore apartments?

L-shaped and straight (one-wall) layouts are best for compact 1 BHK and 2 BHK apartments in Bangalore. They optimize corner space, maximize vertical storage, and keep the central floor area open for movement.

Do you offer custom modular kitchen designs for unique floor plans?

Yes, Sunrise Interiors provides 100% customized modular kitchen designs tailored to your specific floor plan dimensions, plumbing points, electrical outlets, and cooking preferences.

What hardware and fitting brands do you use?

We exclusively use premium, anti-rust hardware from global leaders like Hettich, Blum, and Hafele, including soft-close drawer channels, lift-up systems, and heavy-duty tandem boxes.

Are modular kitchens completely waterproof and termite-proof?

Yes, when built using IS-710 grade BWP Marine Plywood or HDMR core boards with automated zero-gap edge-banding, the cabinets are fully protected against water damage and termite attacks.

What is the difference between a modular kitchen and a civil/carpenter kitchen?

Modular kitchens are factory-manufactured using automated CNC machinery, ensuring precise dimensions, seamless edge-sealing, superior durability, and fast site installation without the mess of traditional carpentry.

What warranty do you provide on modular kitchen cabinetry and hardware?

Sunrise Interiors offers a 5-year structural warranty on core cabinet materials (BWP Plywood) along with up to 10-year manufacturer warranties on selected soft-close hardware and fittings.

Can I integrate built-in appliances like chimneys, hobs, and microwave ovens?

Yes, our 3D layouts include custom tall units and dedicated cutouts designed to seamlessly fit built-in hobs, high-suction chimneys, microwave/OTG units, and dishwashers.

You can request a free consultation by visiting our Nagarbhavi experience center, calling us directly, or submitting your floor plan through our website to connect with an expert kitchen designer near you.
