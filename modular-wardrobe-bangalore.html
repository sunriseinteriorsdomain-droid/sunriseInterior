FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Which material is best for modular wardrobes in Bangalore?

BWP plywood or quality HDMR boards with proper edge-banding perform well in Bangalore’s climate and resist warping better than low-grade particle board.

Sliding or hinged—what should I choose?

Sliding suits smaller rooms with limited clearance; hinged suits larger rooms where you want full open access to every section.

Can wardrobes be floor-to-ceiling?

Yes. We design full-height units with lofts for suitcases and seasonal items, matching your ceiling height and beam positions.

Do you provide internal accessories?

Yes—pull-outs, jewellery trays, trouser racks, and custom organisers based on your storage needs.

How long does wardrobe installation take?

After design freeze, factory production and on-site fitting typically follow a planned schedule of a few weeks depending on scope.

Can wardrobes match my bedroom furniture?

Absolutely. Finishes and handles are coordinated with bed back panels, side tables, and overall bedroom interiors.
