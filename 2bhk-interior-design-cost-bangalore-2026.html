Average 2BHK Interior Design Cost in Bangalore at a Glance

For a standard 2BHK apartment in Bangalore ranging between 800 sq. ft. and 1,200 sq. ft. of built-up area, a complete interior project generally falls into three budget tiers:

Package Tier Estimated Cost Range (Excl. GST) Ideal For Key Inclusions
Essential / Budget ₹3.50 Lakhs – ₹5.50 Lakhs First-time buyers, rental properties, budget-focused setups Modular kitchen, 2 standard wardrobes, TV console unit, basic hardware.
Signature / Mid-Range ₹5.50 Lakhs – ₹9.00 Lakhs Primary homeowners seeking balanced comfort & durability BWP ply kitchen, floor-to-ceiling wardrobes with lofts, TV panel, false ceiling, cove lighting.
Luxury / Premium ₹9.00 Lakhs – ₹15.00 Lakhs+ High-end living, custom architectural elements Premium acrylic/PU/veneer finishes, soft-close Blum hardware, custom lighting, wall panelling, full turnkey civil work.

SEO Insight: Interior design costs are calculated based on the actual square footage of joinery and surface work executed, rather than the total carpet area of the flat. On average, complete turnkey home interiors in Bangalore range from ₹400 to ₹1,600 per sq. ft. of carpet area, depending on finish specifications.

2BHK Interior Design Packages in Bangalore: What Do You Get?

When reviewing 2BHK interior design packages in Bangalore, it helps to understand what each price point delivers so you can align your choices with your personal budget.

2BHK Scope of work breakdown: Modular kitchen, bedroom storage, living & dining zone, decorative services.

1. Essential Tier (Functional Focus)

  • Modular Kitchen: Commercial MR/BWR plywood carcass with HPL shutters and standard hardware.
  • Bedrooms: Two hinged-door wardrobes with basic shelving and top lofts.

  • Hardware: Reliable domestic or OEM hardware from brands like Ebco or Olive.

2. Signature Tier (Turnkey Value - Most Popular)

  • Modular Kitchen: IS:710 grade BWP marine plywood carcass with acrylic or HDHMR shutters.
  • Bedrooms: Floor-to-ceiling sliding door wardrobes with lofts, drawer organizers, and mirrors.
  • Living & Dining: Feature wall TV unit, shoe cabinet, and dining partition.
  • Ceiling & MEP: Gypsum false ceiling with LED cove lighting and branded hardware.

3. Luxury Tier (Bespoke Execution)

  • Modular Kitchen: Premium BWP ply or aluminium carcass, glass profile cabinets, quartz/granite countertops.
  • Bedrooms: Walk-in or floor-to-ceiling wardrobes with lacquered glass or PU finishes.
  • Living & Dining: Stone/veneer wall panelling, storage seating, profile lighting, and smart home points.
  • Full Turnkey Civil Work: Complete bathroom vanity, electrical modifications, accent painting, and wallpaper.

Core Factors Influencing Your 2BHK Interior Cost

Understanding the components of your quotation prevents cost inflation during site execution.

Key cost drivers: board grade, shutter finish, hardware fit, scope of work.

1. Board Material Grade

  • Commercial MR Plywood: Lowest cost; suitable only for dry areas.
  • HDHMR: Excellent smooth finish for dry-zone wardrobes and TV units.
  • BWP IS:710 Marine Plywood: Essential for wet areas like modular kitchens and bathroom vanities.

2. Exterior Shutter Finishes

  • High-Pressure Laminate (1mm): Durable and cost-effective.
  • Acrylic Finishes: Premium mirror-like sheen, UV-resistant and anti-scratch.
  • PU (Polyurethane) Polish & Veneer: High-end handcrafted luxury finishes.

What Most 2BHK Quotes Leave Out: Hidden Costs to Budget For

  1. 18% Statutory GST: Most preliminary estimates list prices before GST.
  2. Kitchen Countertop & Civil Work: Cutting, fitting, and edge-polishing granite or quartz adds ₹25,000 – ₹60,000.
  3. Electrical & Plumbing Point Shifts: Relocating kitchen points adds ₹15,000 – ₹40,000.
  4. Kitchen Appliances: Chimneys, hobs, sinks, faucets, and dishwashers are usually purchased separately.
  5. Deep Cleaning & Site Protection: professional post-installation cleaning and society lift/hallway masking.

5 Smart Ways to Reduce Your 2BHK Interior Cost Without Sacrificing Quality

  1. Mix Board Materials Wisely: Use BWP plywood for wet kitchen cabinets and HDHMR for bedrooms.
  2. Select Laminates for Bedroom Interiors: Save acrylic finishes for kitchen and living room highlights.
  3. Choose Hinged Wardrobes Over Sliding Systems: Hinged doors cost 20% to 30% less.
  4. Opt for Peripheral False Ceilings: Keep border cove ceilings to reduce material costs.
  5. Partner with Direct Factory Manufacturers: Eliminate middleman vendor markups.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: How much does 2BHK interior design cost in Bangalore?

A complete 2BHK home interior in Bangalore generally costs between ₹3.5 Lakhs and ₹5.5 Lakhs for essential setups, ₹5.5 Lakhs to ₹9 Lakhs for mid-range turnkey packages, and ₹9 Lakhs to ₹15 Lakhs+ for luxury customized interiors.

Q2: What is the cost of interiors for a 2BHK flat in Bangalore per square foot?

On average, 2BHK interior design costs range from ₹400 to ₹1,600 per sq. ft. of built-up carpet area, depending on materials, finishes, and civil work requirements.

Q3: What is included in affordable 2BHK interior design packages in Bangalore?

Essential budget packages typically include a modular kitchen setup, two wardrobes with lofts, a wall-mounted TV unit, essential hardware, and basic installation services.

Q4: What is the average 2BHK modular kitchen and wardrobe cost in Bangalore?

Combining a modular kitchen with two bedroom wardrobes generally accounts for 60% to 70% of your woodwork budget, ranging between ₹2.8 Lakhs and ₹5.5 Lakhs for quality materials.

Q5: Can I execute a 2BHK interior design project on a budget under ₹5 Lakhs?

Yes. A budget under ₹5 Lakhs can cover essential woodwork, including a modular kitchen, master bedroom wardrobe, guest bedroom wardrobe, and a simple TV console, by using quality laminates and standard hardware.

Q6: What are the best materials for budget-friendly home interiors?

Combining BWP IS:710 Marine Plywood for wet kitchen areas with HDHMR boards for bedroom wardrobes—finished with 1mm high-pressure laminates—offers the best balance of durability and affordability.

Q7: How long does it take to complete 2BHK home interiors in Bangalore?

Factory-manufactured modular interiors typically take 40 to 50 business days from final 3D design approval to on-site assembly and handover.

Q8: Does the interior design quotation include 18% GST?

Initial estimates often omit GST. Always verify that your final contract explicitly includes statutory 18% GST to ensure complete budget transparency.
