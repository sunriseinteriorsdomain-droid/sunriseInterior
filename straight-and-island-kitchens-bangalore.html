Straight (Single-Wall) Modular Kitchens

Why Choose a Straight Kitchen Layout?

A straight modular kitchen positions all cabinets, appliances, and work surfaces along a single wall. It is the ultimate space-saving solution for modern high-rise apartments in Bangalore.

  • Space Maximization: Leaves the rest of the room completely open for dining tables or living room furniture.
  • Streamlined Linear Workflow: Everything—from washing and chopping to cooking—happens in a smooth, continuous horizontal movement.
  • Budget-Friendly Execution: Requires less material and countertop area, making it an economical choice without compromising quality.
  • Vertical Storage Utilization: Paired with floor-to-ceiling overhead cabinets and lofts to maximize storage capacity upward rather than outward.

Island Modular Kitchens

Why Choose an Island Kitchen Layout?

An island modular kitchen features an independent central counter detached from the main cabinet walls. It serves as the aesthetic and functional heart of modern luxury homes.

  • Multifunctional Central Counter: Functions as an extra food preparation surface, breakfast bar with bar stools, or an entertainment hub while cooking.
  • Integrated Hob or Sink Options: The center island can house a built-in cooking hob with an overhead ceiling chimney, or a secondary prep sink.
  • Deep Under-Counter Storage: Equips the island base with soft-close tandem drawers, wine racks, or pull-out appliance storage.
  • Seamless Open-Plan Integration: Perfect for villas and premium flats in Koramangala, Indiranagar, and Sadashivanagar, connecting the kitchen smoothly to the living and dining areas.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the minimum room space required to install an island kitchen in Bangalore?

An island kitchen requires an open floor plan with a minimum clearance walkway of 3.5 to 4 feet (42 to 48 inches) on all sides of the island. This ensures comfortable movement and unhindered opening of cabinet doors and appliances.

Are straight modular kitchens suitable for small 1 BHK or 2 BHK flats?

Yes. Straight kitchens are specifically designed for small or narrow floor plans. By occupying only one wall, they maximize surrounding floor area while keeping all cooking essentials in a streamlined line.

Which core material is best for kitchen cabinets in Indian homes?

IS-710 Grade Boiling Water Proof (BWP) Marine Plywood is the best material. It offers 100% water resistance around sink zones, handles steam from heavy Indian cooking, and prevents termite damage.

Can a cooktop or sink be installed on the center island counter?

Yes. We can route gas piping/electrical connections or plumbing lines to the center island to install a built-in hob (with a ceiling-mounted island chimney) or a dedicated prep sink.

How long does it take to manufacture and install a straight or island modular kitchen?

Factory manufacturing takes 25 to 30 days post 3D design approval. On-site installation, countertop fitting, and hardware calibration typically take 3 to 5 business days.

What warranty do you provide on straight and island modular kitchens?

Sunrise Interiors provides a 5-year structural warranty on all BWP Plywood cabinetry, along with manufacturer warranties up to 10 years on soft-close European hardware fittings from Hettich, Blum, and Hafele.

Can a straight kitchen be upgraded with a dining or breakfast counter?

Yes. In open-plan apartments, a parallel floating breakfast ledge or fold-down wall table can be added opposite a straight kitchen to double as a dining surface.

How do factory-made kitchens compare to carpenter-built kitchens?

Factory-made modular units use automated CNC cutting and zero-gap edge-banding. This ensures exact fitting, clean seals against moisture penetration, higher scratch resistance, and fast installation without dust or mess at your home.

Do you customize cabinet heights according to user preference?

Yes. Countertop heights and wall cabinet placements are 100% customized based on the primary cook's height to ensure ergonomic comfort during daily cooking.
