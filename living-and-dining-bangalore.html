Solutions

Living & Dining Solutions We Design

Popular configurations for Bangalore apartments and independent homes:

1. TV Unit & Living Wall

Floor-to-ceiling or floating units with panels, niches, and closed storage for a clean media wall.

2. Open-Plan Living–Dining

Shared visual flow with defined dining table placement and subtle dividers when needed.

3. Crockery & Dining Storage

Glass or solid shutters, drawers for cutlery, and display for serveware.

4. Partition & Accent Details

Fluted panels, shelves, or low partitions that separate without closing the room.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you design living and dining together in a small apartment?

Yes. We specialise in open-plan zoning that keeps circulation clear while fitting seating and dining comfortably.

Do you build custom TV units?

Yes—custom sizes with wire management, niches, and closed storage tailored to your TV and devices.

What about crockery unit designs?

We design crockery and buffet units based on your tableware volume and dining room width.

Will finishes match my kitchen?

We coordinate laminates, handles, and colours across living, dining, and kitchen for a unified home.

Do you handle false ceiling and lighting plans?

We plan lighting layers with your interiors and can coordinate with your false-ceiling contractor.

How do I start?

Book a free consultation or visit our Nagarbhavi experience center with your floor plan.
